Step 1 · Key & chords

Tap a key to hear the note, tap a chord to hear it played. Diatonic to the song's current key and mode.

Step 2 · Beat maker

A quick drum + bass sketch in the chosen style and the song's tempo — enough to write against, not a final mix.

Step 3 · Demo mix

Beat + chords + melody, mixed down
Combines the beat maker, your locked or suggested chords, and the melody sketch into one short audio clip you can download and share. Audio only — a full vertical video export isn't something this page can safely build (that needs real video-encoding infrastructure).

Jam session

Plays your beat, chords, and melody together on a loop
A live mixer for everything you've built in Steps 1–3: the beat, your locked or suggested chord progression, and the melody sketch (if you've generated one), all looping together so you can balance them by ear before rendering a demo. The Beat fader also controls Beat Maker's volume outside of jamming.

Hum to chords

Hum a melody, we'll guess the key
Analyzes pitch right in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Works best with a clear, sustained hum and little background noise. Uses the same mic permission as Recorder — manage it from the Recorder card below if needed.

Chord-aware — it knows your locked or suggested progression and favors notes that actually fit, with real phrasing and rhythm instead of one flat note per syllable. Still a scratch sketch to build from, not a finished vocal.

Performance view

Auto-scrolling lyrics, timed for singing along
Duration defaults from your lyric line count — drag the slider to match your own pace, then hit Start scroll and sing along.
